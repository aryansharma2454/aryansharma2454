I am a passionate **Mobile & Full-Stack Engineer** with a strong focus on building responsive, offline-first mobile applications and enterprise systems. I specialize in the **Flutter** and **Android** ecosystems, crafting scalable architectures, low-latency device integrations (Bluetooth thermal printing, biometrics, stylus/S-Pen input), and intuitive UI/UX.

- 🤝 **Open for:** High-impact engineering roles, open-source collaborations, and innovative product builds
